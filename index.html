Get in touch
What should I expect at my first appointment?

We begin with a conversation, followed by an assessment tailored to your needs. You will leave with clear recommendations, transparent costs and time to decide.

I am anxious about dental visits. Can you help?

Absolutely. Let us know when you book. We can slow the pace, explain each step before it happens and build in breaks whenever you need them.

Do you offer payment plans?

Discuss your treatment and budget with the team. Where suitable, we can talk through staged care and the payment options available at the clinic.

How often should I come in?

For many patients, preventive visits every six months work well. Your dentist will recommend a schedule based on your individual oral health needs.
